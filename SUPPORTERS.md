## Getting on the board

1. Support WorldCradle on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/KeithDude).
2. Message me on Patreon that you'd like to be listed, and the name to show.

Only supporters who ask are listed, under the name they choose. To change your name or come off the
board, message me the same way. The board is updated by hand, so it can take a little while.
