🔒 Trusted certificate

Right now browsers warn that this site is “not private”, because its security certificate was made by this computer itself. A free certificate from Let's Encrypt removes the warning. You need two things first:

  1. A name for this dashboard — a web address that leads to your home, like mygames.example.net. Free services such as No-IP or DuckDNS give you one.
  2. One extra rule on your router: send internet port 80 (TCP) to this computer (), port 8080. Let's Encrypt uses it to check the name really is yours, now and each time the certificate renews — so leave the rule in place.
